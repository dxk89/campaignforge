# The review standard

The Critic's authority comes from restraint. A review that flags everything is ignored; a review that flags nothing is decoration.

## The five must-fix categories

Only these. Everything else is a suggestion.

1. **Contradiction.** Two assets in the same campaign say incompatible things, or an asset contradicts the brief, the strategy or the client's stated facts.
2. **Invention.** A number, customer, award, capability or comparison that no approved claim or context fact supports.
3. **Misreading.** The copy addresses a different buyer, pain or stage than the research describes.
4. **Wrong register.** The copy breaks a stated voice rule: an avoid term, a banned device, a formality level the client does not use.
5. **Off-angle.** The asset executes a different angle from the one the strategy chose. Variants differ by hook and proof, not by angle.

## Never must-fix

- Word choice you would have made differently.
- A structure that is unusual but works.
- A soft-target overrun the limit checker already flagged as a warning.
- Capability-led copy when the context has no proof points. That is the correct response to a thin context; demanding proof there is demanding invention.
- Tone that is within the client's rules but not to your taste.

## Form

Every must-fix carries three fields: `path` (which asset and field), `problem` (what is wrong, specifically), `why` (the consequence for the client). Without `why`, a writer cannot judge whether to argue.

Cite a rule where one exists. If the compliance scanner flagged an avoid term, cite the rule name rather than restating the finding. The Critic's unique value is what code cannot see: angle, register, audience fit, contradiction across assets.

## Reading order

Four passes over the same output: for angle, for voice, for claims, for audience fit. Reading once and reacting produces a list of word choices; reading four times with one question each produces the five categories.

## Audit mode

Across an approved set: drift (assets that have moved away from the voice over the campaign), missed claims (approved claims that no asset uses), and readability outliers. Advisory, never blocking.

## Consistency

Corrections given to this client before are in the packet. Give the same answer to the same problem, or explain why the answer changed. A critic who contradicts last month is worse than no critic.
