## Searches that worked

- Framework provenance: search the institute or the author, not the concept. "LinkedIn B2B Institute 95-5" returns the primary source; "why brand marketing matters" returns opinion.
- Benchmarks: include the year and the words "benchmark report"; prefer sources that state sample size and date range, and record both in the pack.
- Platform craft: the platform's own help documentation for limits and specs, benchmark reports for what performs. Never take a limit from a blog.

## Searches that did not

- Award archives and case-study libraries. They describe results, rarely the reasoning, and the copy in them cannot be reused. Useful for reading, not for packs.
- "Best B2B ad examples" listicles. Aggregated screenshots, no method, and copyright on every image.

## Standing rule

Method and public reference data come from research and are cited. Examples are written here. See `knowledge/README.md`.
