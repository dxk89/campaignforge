# Worked examples: Search Specialist

## Example 1 — one bucket becomes four groups

**Seed terms** (from audience research and autocomplete): reconcile stripe payouts, payout reconciliation software, how to reconcile stripe in xero, month end close automation, best reconciliation tool, stripe fees accounting, ledgerline.

**Grouped.**

| Group | Intent | Keywords | Landing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payout reconciliation — software | transactional | "payout reconciliation software", "reconciliation tool for stripe" | /trial |
| Stripe reconciliation — how | informational | "how to reconcile stripe in xero", "stripe fees accounting" | /guide, not /trial |
| Month-end close — automation | commercial | "month end close automation", "close faster software" | /trial |
| Brand | navigational | "ledgerline", "ledgerline pricing" | / |

**Annotation.**
- The informational group points at a guide, not the trial. Sending "how do I…" traffic to a signup page produces clicks and no conversions, and then the account is blamed for the terms rather than the destination.
- "Best reconciliation tool" went to the commercial group with comparison-shaped copy, not to the transactional group.
- Brand is separate so its cheap conversions do not flatter the non-brand groups' numbers.

## Example 2 — an RSA that respects the format

Group: *Payout reconciliation — software*.

| # | Headline | Chars |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payout Reconciliation Software | 30 |
| 2 | Ledgerline Reconciliation | 25 |
| 3 | Reconcile Stripe Payouts | 24 |
| 4 | Close the Month 4 Days Faster | 29 |
| 5 | Review Exceptions, Not Lines | 28 |
| 6 | Works With Xero and NetSuite | 28 |
| 7 | Free 14-Day Trial, No Card | 26 |
| 8 | Ledgerline for Finance Teams | 28 |

**Annotation.**
- Product name in 2 and 8; group terms in 1, 3 and 6; offer in 7 only. Every line stands alone in any position.
- Nothing pinned. The combinations all read correctly, which is the test.
- Headline 6 names compatible products as a fact. Naming a competitor for comparison would need substantiation and sign-off.

## Example 3 — negatives before launch

**Built before the campaign ran:** jobs, salary, career, internship, free, open source, template, excel, spreadsheet template, tutorial, course, what is, meaning, definition, and the sector homonym "reconciliation" in its legal and religious senses.

**Annotation.**
- "Spreadsheet template" is the interesting one: the product's whole story is about replacing a spreadsheet, so the term is thematically adjacent and commercially worthless. Adjacency is where budget leaks.
- Written before launch. A negative list assembled from the first search-terms report costs a fortnight of spend to compile.

---

## Self-check applied
One intent per group; every RSA headline relevant to its group; product name in two, group terms in three; offer once; negatives cover job seekers, free and education intents; no trademark terms in ad text.
