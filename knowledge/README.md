# Knowledge and examples

Two kinds of file live here.

**Packs** (`*.md`) carry reference material an agent looks up rather than remembers: platform specs, benchmark ranges, framework definitions, legal codes. Each has front matter with a `checked:` date, a `review:` interval and `sources:` at the bottom. A pack older than its interval is flagged on `/prompts`.

**Examples** (`examples.md` per agent) are worked examples: an input, the output, and an annotation saying why each decision was made, plus what a junior would have written instead. They go in the packet, not the role, so they can vary by client and task.

## Rule on sourcing examples

Examples in this repository are **original work written for this repository**, in the style the research describes. We do not copy campaign copy, articles, ad text or creative from other companies, agencies or award archives. Copyright applies to them, and a tool that hands a client someone else's copy is a liability.

What we do take from published sources is **method and public reference data**: framework definitions with citations, platform specifications, benchmark ranges with dates, and the reasoning patterns described in published case studies. Those are cited in the packs. The fictional companies in the examples (Ledgerline, and others added later) exist only here.

Client exemplars, retrieved from approved work, sit alongside these in the packet and are the stronger signal once a client has history. The files here are what a new client starts from.
