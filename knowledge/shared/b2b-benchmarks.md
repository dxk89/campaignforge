# B2B benchmark ranges

Ranges, never single numbers. Every plan and forecast that uses these must state the range and this date. Benchmarks are planning inputs, not targets; a segment-level internal benchmark beats any published average once results exist.

## Paid channels

| Channel | CPC | CPM | CTR | Conversion | CPL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google / Microsoft Search (blended, all industries) | ~$5.40 | — | ~6.6% | ~8.2% | ~$67 blended; up to ~$130 in the most competitive B2B categories |
| LinkedIn Sponsored Content | $5–15 typical B2B SaaS; $3–6 in less competitive sectors | median ~$31; $55–85 broad B2B, $90–150 narrow enterprise, $150–300 ultra-narrow | 0.44–0.65% single image; below 0.35% signals a creative or targeting problem | ~6% (US average, lead forms higher than landing pages) | $75–150 lead-gen form; $100–250 landing page |
| LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads | ~$2.30 median | — | ~2.7% median | — | — |
| LinkedIn Document Ads | — | — | 1.2–2.5% | — | typically 30–40% below generic lead forms |
| Meta (B2B) | lower than LinkedIn | — | — | — | lower CPL, but leads need more nurturing before they are sales-qualified |

Notes for the Media Planner: LinkedIn looks expensive on platform metrics and strong on pipeline metrics; judge it on cost per SQL and pipeline-to-spend, not CPC. Search captures existing intent and generally produces the lowest CPL of the major paid channels. Lead-gen forms convert better and qualify worse than landing pages; choose by whether the campaign needs volume or fit.

## Email

| Metric | Range | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate (nurture) | 25–35%; welcome sequences much higher on email 1 | Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates reported opens by roughly 8–18%; do not forecast from opens |
| Click rate | 2.5–4% typical, 5%+ top performers | The reliable engagement metric |
| Click-to-open | 12–20% B2B | Isolates body from subject |
| Unsubscribe | under 0.5% per send; 0.2–0.4% typical | Above 0.5% means frequency or relevance |
| Bounce | under 2% hard | Deliverability health |
| MQL conversion from a nurture sequence | 6–14% | The sequence-level number that matters |

Practice notes: single-CTA emails outperform multi-link emails on click rate; subject lines of roughly 6–10 words fit the mobile preview; SPF, DKIM and DMARC are prerequisites, not optimisations; sunset contacts with no engagement after 90 days.

## Using these

1. Forecast as a range and label it with this date.
2. Prefer the client's own history the moment it exists; these are for the first campaign only.
3. State the segment. A nurture email to demo requesters and a cold newsletter share no benchmark.
4. Never present a benchmark as a target without saying what would have to be true to hit it.
