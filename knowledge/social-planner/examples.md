# Worked examples: Social Planner

---

## Example 1 — a week that carries the angle without advertising it

Lead angle: *Four days back*. Series device: **Close Notes**, a Tuesday number.

| Day | Channel | Pillar | Post (opening line) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LinkedIn | educate | "Pull the payout reports before you open the bank feed. Match in that order and the fees stop going missing." | Useful with no product in it. Earns the right to the rest of the week |
| 2 | X | engage | "Finance leads: what day of the month do you actually close? Reply with the number." | Invites the audience's own data, which becomes a later post |
| 3 | Instagram | proof | Stat card: four days, with the survey source in the caption | The only claim of the week, sourced |
| 3 | LinkedIn | point-of-view | "The recon spreadsheet is not a process. It is a person. When she is on holiday, you do not have a process." | The angle, argued rather than asserted |
| 5 | X | educate | "Stripe fees are not a black box. They are a column you have not exported yet." | Correction hook, complete under 280 |

**Annotation.**
- One product mention across five posts, and it is the proof card. The angle is present in every post through the *idea*, not the pitch.
- Day 2's question is designed to produce material: the replies become a later educate post about the spread of close dates. A month should feed itself.
- The point-of-view post takes a position that could be argued with. A post nobody could disagree with is not a point of view.
- Openings vary: instruction, question, claim, aphorism, correction.

## Example 2 — a launch on day 2

**Calendar.** Product launch on day 2. A naive plan announces it on day 2 and moves on.

**Plan.** Day 1 LinkedIn (educate) on the problem the launch addresses, no mention of a launch. Day 2 LinkedIn (product) the announcement. Day 2 Instagram (product) announce card. Day 4 X (educate) one thing the new capability changes in practice. Day 8 LinkedIn (proof) the first thing a customer did with it, if a claim exists; otherwise educate on a use case.

**Annotation.**
- Two posts of build, the announcement, then follow-through. An announcement with nothing around it reaches the people who happened to be looking that day.
- Day 4 answers "so what", which the announcement never does well.
- Day 8 is conditional on an approved claim existing. The plan says what happens if it does not, rather than assuming one will appear.

## Example 3 — no proof points

**Context.** `proof_points: []`.

**Change.** The proof pillar's 20% is reallocated: 15% to educate, 5% to point-of-view. `pillars` records: "proof pillar not used this month; no approved claims in the client context. Reallocated to educate."

**Annotation.**
- The reallocation is stated, not silent, so the client sees the cost of having no approved proof.
- A junior writes "trusted by finance teams" and fills the slot. That single line is the difference between a tool that can be trusted unread and one that cannot.

## Example 4 — X post that fails the count

**Draft.** 268 characters plus three hashtags at 34 = 302. Over.

**Fix.** Not truncation. The second sentence was the same idea as the first, so it goes; the post lands at 214 plus tags. Cutting the *idea* rather than the words is the discipline; truncating produces a post that stops rather than ends.

## Self-check applied
Shares within tolerance; every hook stands alone; X posts complete with tags under 280; every Instagram post has a graphic; one idea per pillar per week; blackout dates respected; series device named.
